## Findings

- **[severity: bug]** research/minimal_network.md:56-63 (table "cuts removed by lifts | cuts added above a lift", and "SETN's 6 removed cuts gave 601 new ones, 592 of them on lines with no cut before") — these are positions in `gradient_barriers_minimal`, not cuts. lifts.R's `pos()`/`only()` count distinct rows of that table; many of them sit at a line mouth or on an FWA segment end, where `frs_break_apply()` adds nothing. Measured on the reproduced counterfactual: of SETN's 601 "added" positions, 258 lie within 1 m of an FWA segment start (150 at measure < 1, a line mouth); BULK 8 of 79. lifts.csv itself shows it: SETN positions net +595 (10,771 vs 10,176) but segments net only +335 (72,354 vs 72,019); BULK +72 vs +65. The same write-up's census (breaks_census.csv) counts real cuts by source and gives BULK 6,414 gradient_minimal cuts where lifts.csv says `cuts_built` 8,057, so "cuts" means two different things on one page. Lifts add about 343 cuts in SETN, not 601, and "592 on lines with no cut before" overstates too (those lines carry FWA segment ends; many of the 592 are mouth positions). Fix: count added/removed positions after excluding those within 1 m of an existing segment start in the other build (or report segment-count deltas, which lifts.csv already holds), or rename the columns "minimal barrier positions".
