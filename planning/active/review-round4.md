## Findings

1. **Row 2, "lake and wetland rearing habitat by area" (DESCRIPTION and README line 5).** Area is not the criterion for every species. CO lakes and wetlands have no area minimum in `default` (`default/rules.yaml:120-165`). CO's 1050/1150 wetland-flow rearing has no area test in either config (`thresholds: false`, no `wetland_ha_min`; `bcfishpass/rules.yaml:57-82`). In `default`, lakes and wetlands also need spawning within 10 km. Suggested wording: "and lake and wetland rearing habitat by size and, in `default`, connection to spawning", or "lake and wetland reaches as rearing habitat".

2. **Row 3, "marks each of those reaches with the kind of human-made barrier below it: a road or rail crossing, or a dam" (DESCRIPTION and README line 5).**
   - It drops the "if any" that the Connectivity paragraph has. Reaches with no anthropogenic barrier downstream are `NONE` (`R/lnk_pipeline_mapping_code.R:203,210`).
   - Add "if any" in both places.

3. **Row 6, "a crossing modelled, assessed or remediated as a barrier" (README line 48).**
   - `REMEDIATED` marks a remediated crossing, `pscis_status = 'REMEDIATED' AND barrier_status = 'PASSABLE'` (`R/lnk_barriers_emit.R:38-39`). That is a former barrier made passable, not a barrier, and the sentence presents it as one kind of human-made barrier.
   - Suggested: "a crossing modelled or assessed as a barrier, a dam, or a crossing that has been remediated".
   - Row 3's list ("a road or rail crossing, or a dam") also leaves the remediated case out, which is acceptable once "if any" is added.

4. **Row 5, "its phases are also exported as `lnk_pipeline_*()` functions" (README line 46, minor).**
   - The run also chains `lnk_persist_init()`, `lnk_barriers_unify()`, `lnk_barriers_views()`, `lnk_presence()` and `lnk_mapping_code()` (`R/lnk_pipeline_run.R:211-303`).
   - `lnk_pipeline_access()` depends on views that only `lnk_barriers_views()` builds.
   - Suggested: "its phases are also exported functions (`lnk_pipeline_*()` and the `lnk_barriers_*()` steps) that can be run and inspected one at a time".

5. **Row 13, "configs built on `default` inherit them" (README line 75, minor).**
   - Only `default_tuned` inherits.
   - `default_extrabreaks` and `default_rearbreaks` are `default` variants with `extends: ~`. They carry their own copies, last committed 2026-05-04, and five of the seven differ from `default`'s.
   - Suggested: "`default_tuned` inherits them; the experimental `default_extrabreaks` and `default_rearbreaks` carry their own unsynced copies". Or drop "configs built on `default`" for "`default_tuned` inherits them".
