## Findings

- **[fragile] RUNBOOK.md §2c "The one access change" ("never between 0 and open"), and pre_post_diff.R:11-12 (expected: "no 0 <-> {1, 2} … no has_barriers change").** The claim does not always hold. Observations are the first break source, and `frs_break_apply()` cuts later sources only more than 1 m from the current segment ends. So under `all`, a natural barrier G within 1 m of an observation cut R (one that is not a top) does not cut. The segment starting at R reads G as downstream (`fwa_downstream`, include_equivalents, tolerance 1, `abs(drm_a - drm_b) <= 1`). Under `top_per_species`, R is gone and G cuts at its own position. So the boundary moves by up to 1 m:
  - G = R + 0.8: [R, G] goes from 0 to open.
  - G = R − 0.8: [G, R] goes from open to 0.

  `has_barriers_<sp>_dnstr` flips on that sliver too. The same absorption applies to crossings, which move mapping-code tokens on ≤ 1 m slivers. Crossings are where fish are often sampled. pre_post_diff.R drops only pieces under 1 cm, so these slivers will show as non-zero `km_acc_0_to_open` / `km_acc_open_to_0` / `km_has_barriers_change`, against the stated expectation. Either the doc and the expectation name this case (a ≤ 1 m boundary shift where a later source was absorbed by a removed observation cut), or the diff classifies those pieces separately. I checked "never 2 → 1" and it does hold: every species recorded at a removed position has its top at or above R + 1, and that top is either a cut or still upstream of G.

- **[bug, measurement] data-raw/logs/observation_breaks_396/province.sql:9-10 (`tops … GROUP BY blk, sp`).** The pipeline takes tops per group, because `<schema>.observations` is AOI-filtered on the observation's `watershed_group_code` (`.lnk_pipeline_prep_observations()`). This script takes them once per line across the whole province. A `blue_line_key` that spans groups (every inter-group main stem) gets one top per species here but one per group per species in a build. So `tops` and `pct_kept` (45.4 % in province.csv) understate what `default` keeps, while the README presents it as "the same ratio province-wide". Group by `watershed_group_code, blk, sp`; `obs_province` carries the column.

- **[bug, measurement, small] data-raw/logs/observation_breaks_396/cuts.sql:48-49,61-62 (`near_top`, used in `removed_saving_a_segment`).** The script assumes a removed position within 1 m of a kept top saves no segment. In `frs_break_apply()` all observation breakpoints are joined against the segment's original ends in one call (`to_break`, then `lead()`), so a top and a non-top 1 m apart both cut, and dropping the non-top does save one segment. The column therefore undercounts and disagrees with `positions_cutting − tops_cutting`, which models fresh correctly: BULK 552 against 550, ELKR 1,426 against 1,423 (PARS and LKEL agree). Drop the `NOT near_top` term.
