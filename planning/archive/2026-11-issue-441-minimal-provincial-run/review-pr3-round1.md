- **[bug]** `research/bcfishpass_parity.md` ("These are not decomposed segment by segment: under #326 the two builds share few segments … so `segment_diff.csv` covers little") — the committed evidence contradicts this. In the four groups the new build has 129,329 segments (`moves.log`) and 34,223 of them are new-only (`resegmented.csv.gz`, side `only_361`), so 95,106 (74 %) are shared. 195,329 is the sum of both sides' one-build-only segments, and the old build alone contributes 161,106. `segment_diff.csv` does decompose the moves. On shared segments, rearing gains match the pair moves (BULK BT net +22.1 km against +21.4; BABL BT +27.3 against +23.3; BABL ST +25.5 against +18.2; the gap is lake connection lines, which segment_diff counts and the score does not). Spawning changed on **zero** km of shared segments in every group and species, while `resegmented_habitat.csv` holds the spawning moves (ADMS BT 347.8 → 343.4, ELKR BT −8.2, ELKR WCT −9.1). That split is the evidence the two causal bullets lack: rearing moved on unchanged geometry (a rule change, consistent with the order-1 exception), and spawning moved only where lines were re-broken. As written, both attributions read as unmeasured, and the data that would measure them is called useless.
  - **Rule:** code-check.md "The probe is broken before the world is" ("Before attributing a difference"); "A proxy is not the property"
