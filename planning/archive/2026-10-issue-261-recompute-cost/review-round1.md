(CLAUDE.md's "33 of 33 walks matched, 3.6× to 24.6× faster" also reads as a per-walk range; the per-walk range in the TSV is 1.14× to 27.5×, and 3.6–24.6× is the per-group total excluding SETN, which research/recompute_cost.md states correctly.)

- **[bug] CLAUDE.md:16, README.md:73-75, research/recompute_cost.md:27-28 and :35 — the "probe costs 9×" figure and the 20× decomposition are not supported by the plans at the stated precision.** 0.001 and 0.009 ms are the GiST Bitmap Index Scan node's per-loop `Actual Total Time`, which EXPLAIN rounds to 3 decimals, so SALR's 0.001 is anything in 0.0005–0.0015 ms (3.6–10.9 s over 7,240,975 loops, a 6× to 18× ratio, not "7 s against 65 s"). The inner side measured without rounding (Nested Loop total minus the outer Index Scan) is 19,581.5 − 6,600.9 = 12,981 ms in SALR and 80,120.6 − 5,690.5 = 74,430 ms in CHWK: 5.7×. And research line 35 ("CHWK costs about 20× SALR because its probe costs about 9× and it walks 8 natural views to SALR's 2") multiplies to 36×; what the data reconciles is per CH walk 80.3 / 19.6 s = 4.1× times 48.5 M / 10.7 M walked rows = 4.5×, ≈ 18.4×, which is the measured natural-walk ratio (545.7 / 29.7 s in walks.tsv). The 6 s fixed outer scan is a third of SALR's walk, which is why a probe-only ratio cannot be multiplied by the view count.

- **[bug] CLAUDE.md:20 and research/recompute_cost.md:42 — "Both are btree lookups" is contradicted by the committed prefilter plans.** `plans/prefilter_SETN_barriers_ch_access.json` and `prefilter_CHWK_barriers_ch_access.json` serve the `wscode_ltree = ANY (ARRAY(...))` arm with a Bitmap Index Scan on `barriers_wscode_gist_idx` (GiST); only the `blue_line_key` arm uses the btree `barriers_blk_drm_idx`. - **[bug] README.md:47-50 and :123, :139 — the contention statements do not match the rows.** README says "later scripts accept one other backend" and "the pool saving is an estimate from uncontended walks". `predictors.tsv` records `others_active` 8 for 19 groups and 6 for one (UKEC through ZYMO, the alphabetical tail), i.e. their `probe_us` / `probe_pre_us` were timed beside 6–8 other active backends; `predictors.R` checks `others() > 1` only once, before the loop. `walk_prefilter.tsv` has three SALR rows at 2. The effect on the headline is small (Spearman of predicted walk seconds is 0.982 over all 240 and 0.982 over the 220 quiet groups; the 20 contribute 2,760 of 33,398 predicted s), but UKEC's prefiltered sample walk is slower than its sent one (7.16 vs 5.45 µs), so those rows are noisy inputs to the pool range and the prose should say they were contended. Separately, the LSKE sentence ("67.6 s with no other backend and 68.0 to 70.1 s beside one") picks the minimum: the three uncontended CH-shape LSKE walks are 67.6, 68.4 and 69.2 s, so the data shows no contention effect rather than a small one.

- **[fragile] data-raw/logs/recompute_cost_261/rerun.R:75-82 — a second invocation destroys the backup the first one told the operator to restore from.** Each run does `DROP TABLE IF EXISTS zz261_backup.<t>_<wsg>` and re-copies the group's *current* rows before computing `d0`. If a run ends at lines 160-166 (digest moved: it prints `restore: … SELECT * FROM zz261_backup.<t>_<wsg>` and quits 1, keeping the backups), re-running the script (say, to retry) overwrites those backups with the moved rows, takes `d0` from the moved state, and if the second run reproduces itself, its digests match and lines 168-170 drop the backups: the pre-run rows are then gone. Likewise an error mid-run (lock_timeout, statement_timeout, the downstream guard raising) leaves the backups but writes no digest file and no restore line, and the next run overwrites them. It did not bite here (both digests unchanged), but it is the one write path on the branch; refusing to start when `zz261_backup.<t>_<wsg>` already exists (or suffixing a run stamp) closes it.
